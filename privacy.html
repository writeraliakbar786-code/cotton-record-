Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cotton Record may collect, use and protect information when you use our website and application.

By using Cotton Record, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may process information such as:

  • Name and username used for your account.
  • Information required to authenticate your account.
  • Cotton record names, weights and rates entered by you.
  • Images uploaded for record processing.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the service.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide cotton record management features.
  • Process uploaded record images.
  • Calculate weights and payments.
  • Maintain application security.
  • Improve reliability and functionality.
  • Process premium subscriptions when applicable.

Uploaded Images

If you upload notebook or record images, those images may be processed by the application's image-processing service to extract information requested by you.

Do not upload information that you do not have permission to process. Users should also avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Cookies and Local Storage

Cotton Record may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to remember application preferences and certain application data.

Third-party services, including advertising services if enabled, may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled on Cotton Record, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize, measure or improve advertisements, subject to their respective policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the service. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

Cotton Record may use third-party services for functions such as authentication, image processing, hosting, payments or advertising. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
