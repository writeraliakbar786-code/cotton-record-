What to Include in a Support Request

To help us understand and resolve your problem more quickly, please include:

  • A short description of the problem.
  • The page or feature where the problem occurred.
  • Any relevant error message.
  • Your device or browser information when necessary.
  • Your username if the issue is related to your account.
Security: Never send your password, payment card number, API key, verification code or other secret credentials through email or phone support.
