About Cotton Record

Cotton Record is a web-based record management application designed to help users organize cotton purchase records, weights, names and payment calculations in one place.

The application is designed with simplicity in mind so that users can manage records from a mobile phone or computer without depending on paper calculations alone.

What Cotton Record Does

  • Manage cotton buyer or supplier names.
  • Select multiple notebook record pictures.
  • Process record images and extract available record information.
  • Review and correct detected names and weights.
  • Calculate payment according to the entered rate.
  • Combine records belonging to the same name.
  • Export saved results.
  • Print final summaries.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make cotton record management easier, faster and more organized, especially for users who traditionally maintain their records in notebooks or handwritten registers.

Accuracy Notice

Image-based record processing may not always be completely accurate, especially when handwriting is unclear, images are low quality, or numbers are difficult to read.

Users should always review and verify extracted names and weights before saving or using the final payment calculation.
